# Prometheus Open

**Open-source energy and operations software for any business.**

## The energy-hub thesis
Owner-operated hotels, restaurants, retail shops and laundromats lose real money on energy they can't see. Prometheus Open starts with the equipment that costs the most to run and is the hardest to watch: water heaters and HVAC. Stick a sensor on, open your phone, and see a number. You get live alerts when something drifts and the savings shown in dollars, from the day it's unboxed. There are no long installs and no sales engineers.

It's built local-first on proven open-source foundations such as Home Assistant, so your data stays on your property and keeps working when the internet doesn't.

## The suite vision
Energy is the first module. The same phone-first, open-source backbone grows into a suite of operations tools:

- **Energy hub**: water heater and HVAC monitoring, alerts and savings. *Available as a pilot.*
- **Inventory**: photo-to-inventory and par levels from a phone. *Available as a pilot.*
- **Procurement**: ordering and invoice capture without the spreadsheet. *Available as a pilot.*
- **Coming:** grocery ordering, direct booking, and airport-hotel tools.

## Why open source
- **No vendor lock-in.** You own the code and your data.
- **It outlives us.** If we disappeared tomorrow, your system would keep running.
- **Costs stay near zero.** You're paying for results, not licenses.
